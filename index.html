Privacy

The game works without an account. If you never sign in, your progress stays in this browser and the game sends none of it anywhere.

On your device

Crowns, Armory items, best runs, levels won, a saved run and your settings are kept in this browser's storage. Clearing the site's data deletes them.

If you sign in with Google

Sign-in and cloud saves run on Google Firebase. It stores your Google name, email address and a user ID, plus a copy of your progress so you can continue on another device. Firebase also logs your IP address and browser details for a few weeks to protect sign-in from abuse. The game never sees your password, and only you can read or change your save.

What the game doesn't do

No ads, analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared. The website you play on has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

While signed in, tap Delete cloud save on the title screen. It removes your cloud save and sign-in record and leaves this device's progress alone.

Children

The game isn't aimed at children under 13. They should play without signing in.

Delete your cloud save?

This removes your progress and sign-in record from the cloud. Your progress on this device stays.

Which progress do you want to keep?

This device and your Google account have different progress. The one you don't keep is replaced.
